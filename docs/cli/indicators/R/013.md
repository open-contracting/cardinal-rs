# High use of non-competitive methods (R013)

The proportion of contracting processes for a buyer using non-competitive methods is a high outlier.

## Methodology

For each buyer, the **ratio** is calculated as $numberOfNonCompetitiveProcesses \over numberOfProcesses$ across all contracting processes. A buyer is flagged if its ratio is greater than or equal to the **upper fence** of $Q_3 + 1.5(IQR)$, where $Q_3$ is the third quartile and $IQR$ is the interquartile range for the set of ratios. If a **threshold** is configured, it is used instead of the upper fence.

For each procuring entity, the **ratio** is calculated the same as for buyers.

A contracting process is non-competitive if its `/tender/procurementMethod` is 'direct' or 'limited'.

A contracting process without a `/tender/procurementMethod` is excluded from both the numerator and the denominator, so that incomplete reporting of procurement methods cannot dilute a buyer's ratio.

:::{admonition} Example
:class: seealso

25% ($Q_1$) of buyers in Atlantis award at most 0% of contracting processes directly. 75% ($Q_3$) of buyers award at most 20% directly. This yields an upper fence of 50%.

The State Water Corporation awarded 60% of its contracting processes directly. This is more than the upper fence of 50%. Therefore, the State Water Corporation is flagged.
:::

:::{admonition} Why is this a red flag?
:class: hint

While there are valid reasons to use non-competitive procurement methods – where only a select list of suppliers can participate, or the contracting process is awarded directly without competition – these methods are by default less competitive and transparent.
:::

:::{admonition} Interpret with care
:class: caution

Entire sectors are lawfully exempt from competition. Defense, security and sole-source maintenance of proprietary systems are the common cases, and a buyer in such a sector can legitimately award every contract directly. Review `/tender/procurementMethodDetails` and the buyer's remit before drawing a conclusion, and consider configuring {ref}`exclusions<indicators-exclusions>` for the procedures that your jurisdiction exempts.
:::
